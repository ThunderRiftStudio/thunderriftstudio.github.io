Sanctions Policy

This page describes the warning system and the sanctions applied in the event of violations of our EULA & Terms of Service. All sanctions are applied at the sole discretion of the Publisher and may be cumulative.

For any questions or appeals: contact starsgamesent@gmail.com

We are committed to providing a fun and fair experience for all players, and we consider our role in detecting and addressing violations to be vital to maintaining a level and enjoyable playing field for everyone.

Before accessing and playing ThunderRift Studio games, you agreed to the game's EULA and Terms of Service. This Security and Enforcement Policy outlines in greater detail the actions on your part that are subject to sanctions, as well as the types of penalties that may apply—ranging from temporary bans to the termination of your license.

1. Warning System

Each confirmed infraction results in one or more warnings being added to the offending account. Warnings accumulate permanently and trigger automatic sanctions upon reaching the thresholds below. The Publisher reserves the right to skip thresholds in the event of a severe or deliberate infraction.

Warning thresholds and associated sanctions:

2
IP Block — 24 hours Temporary connection block from the game's online services.
3
IP Block — 7 days Extended Temporary connection block from the game's online services.
5
Hardware bans — 7 days A Hardware-level ban. No new account can be created/login from this device during the period.
7
Hardware bans — 30 days + Possible account deletion Extended Hardware-level ban. The account may be permanently deleted.
8
Hardware bans — 365 days + Account deletion (99%) More Extended Hardware-level ban. Account deletion is near-certain.

IP blocks affect the connection address used at the time of the infraction. HWID bans are tied to the hardware identifier of the device used and persist regardless of the account or IP address used.

Please note that these sanctions will apply to all ThunderRift Studio games that use online servers (TPlayConnect).

2. Infractions & Warning Values

The following infractions are recognized by the Publisher. Each infraction carries a defined number of warnings upon confirmed detection. The Publisher reserves the right to adjust the number of warnings applied depending on the severity or context of the violation.

Infraction Category Warnings
Harassment, griefing or spamming other players Gameplay +1
Malicious Reporting of other players Gameplay +1
Deliberate exploitation of bugs or technical flaws Gameplay +1

Use of bots or unauthorized macros Anti-Cheat +3

Bypassing, disabling, or targeted attacks on servers Server +8
Creating a new account to circumvent an existing ban Account +8
Bypassing, disabling, or tampering with the Anti-Cheat system Anti-Cheat +8

Infractions marked with an outlined badge are directly related to the Anti-Cheat or account integrity and are treated with higher priority.

Note that warnings adding +5 points can, if repeated, result in enough points to trigger a new sanction, rather than simply adding another 5 points automatically.

3. Appeals

Any player who believes a sanction has been applied in error may submit an appeal via the official Discord server. The Publisher undertakes to review any dispute made in good faith within 14 business days. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee the lifting of the sanction.

Appeals must include: the account username, the Discord ID, the date the sanction was applied, and a description of the situation. Incomplete or bad-faith appeals will not be considered.
