What is Abriph™

Abriph™ is ThunderRift Studio's proprietary anti-cheat system, built to protect our games and ensure fair play for every player.

Is it secure?

As of the time this page was created (July 3, 2026), Abriph™ had been in development for seven months. We previously had an anti-cheat program called Warfare Anti-Cheat™, but it lagged significantly behind our new system, which is capable of detecting most cheat software that aims to affect the experience of other players.

The Game incorporates a cheat detection and prevention system (hereinafter "the Anti-Cheat"), which is active from the moment the Game is launched. By installing and using the Game, you expressly consent to the activation and operation of this system.

Operation and data analyzed:

As part of its function, the Anti-Cheat may:

  • analyze active processes, services, drivers on your system during the game session to detect any cheating software, memory injection, or Game manipulation;
  • collect technical identifiers regarding your hardware (hardware id, ip adress, system infos) for detection and persistent banning purposes;
  • transmit anonymized technical information regarding detected anomalies to our servers.

The Anti-Cheat does not access any personal data unrelated to the integrity of the Game (documents, personal files, browsing history, etc.).
